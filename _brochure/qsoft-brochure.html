Why Q'SOFT®

Take the stress out of waiting.

The challenge

Customers queueing for hours. Complaints about who is next. Staff hastily doing their job while the waiting area fills up. The result is a frustrating, stressful service experience for everyone involved.

The Q'SOFT® answer

A customised queue solution that offers control and flexibility in managing queue flow — digital tokens, appointments, live displays and notifications, backed by reporting that shows where time is being lost.

Core capabilities

Digital tokens & appointments

Token generation at kiosks and terminals, plus internet-based appointment scheduling.

Real-time monitoring

Live queue status on displays, with SMS and app notifications for customers.

Analytics & administration

Centralised administration with queue and staff performance reports.

Finds the bottlenecks

Capable of identifying current and potential future bottlenecks.

Integrates with your systems

Works with third-party software and systems to increase productivity by reducing process steps.

Simple installation

Quick to install, yet packed with powerful and comprehensive features.
