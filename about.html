Who we are

Group expertise, delivered locally.

ATT Systems (India) Private Limited is the India entity of the ATT Systems Group, headquartered in Singapore. The Group has been "Creating The Difference" for its clients through consistent innovation and 24/7/365 customer care, and the India team carries that same mandate — designing, deploying and supporting mission-critical systems for healthcare, government, defence and enterprise clients.

Our portfolio spans Enterprise IT Systems, Integrated Security Management, Traffic Management and the flagship SUBS and Q'SOFT® platforms, alongside system integration, software development and technical support delivered from our India operations base in Karnataka.

"We possess the required experience and technical capability for supply, installation, testing, commissioning and support of similar systems, backed by the necessary technical manpower and infrastructure for successful project implementation."

How we got here

Our journey

A track record built one deployment at a time — from queue management in public healthcare to access control for diplomatic missions.

  • ATT Systems Group foundedThe Group establishes its Singapore headquarters, building Enterprise IT, Security and Traffic Management Systems for government and enterprise clients.
  • 2007 — India operations beginATT Systems (India) Private Limited is established to deliver the Group's portfolio to Indian healthcare, government and enterprise clients.
  • Queue & access deployments across public healthcareQ'SOFT® and access control systems go live across ESIC hospitals and AIIMS Delhi, streamlining patient and visitor flow.
  • Diplomatic & international clientsSecurity and access control work delivered for the Embassy of the Philippines and Singapore Food Agency's visitor management system.
  • SUBS and Q'SOFT® mature into flagship platformsQ'SOFT® achieves registered trademark status (TM No. 2515520) while SUBS, ATT's AI-powered unified command platform, scales into traffic, security and emergency-management deployments.
